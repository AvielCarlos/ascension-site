## How the funnel evolves
1. One test per round. The `FUNNEL` block at the top of the page holds the round name and the variants.
2. Each visitor gets one variant, kept in their browser and written into the URL as `?v=a|b|c`.
3. Events sent to Meta, each tagged with round, variant, creative (utm_content) and source:
   PageView, ViewContent, SongPlay, SongHalf, SongComplete, Scroll50, Lead (any order button), OutboundListen.
4. Read results in Ads Manager with custom conversions "Lead, URL contains v=a" etc. Winner = most Leads per visitor.
5. Keep the winner as `a`, add one or two challengers, rename `round`, note it below.

Ad links carry `?utm_source=meta&utm_content=listener|couple|lyrics`. `couple` swaps the hero photo to match the ad.
